## 3. Cookies and Local Preferences

Google AdSense may use cookies to display advertisements based on previous visits to this or other websites. You can manage personalised advertising in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).

The site uses browser local storage to remember your light or dark theme preference when you move between pages.

## 4. Data Use

The site operator does not sell or trade readers' personal information. Data processed by third-party services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

## 5. Managing Your Preferences

You can restrict or clear cookies and local site data in your browser settings, and manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings. Clearing local site data restores the default theme.
