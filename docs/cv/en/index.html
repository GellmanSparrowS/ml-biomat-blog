Research profile (Chinese) · 中文简历

Research Interests

My research focuses on the rational design of biomaterial systems and structures across scales. I combine physics-informed artificial intelligence, multiscale simulation, and experimental validation to investigate how composition, hierarchical organization, and interactions govern material properties and function. Current work spans structural proteins, fibrous networks, and flexible material systems.
