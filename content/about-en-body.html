His honors include a runner-up finish in the AI for Research Track of the Inaugural World AI Open Source Competition, Second Prize in the Achievement Category of the China Invention Association's Invention and Entrepreneurship Award (third contributor), and the Ruiqing Scholarship.

Research

Biomaterial systems and structures across scales

From molecules and fibers to networks and bulk materials, this research examines how composition, hierarchical structure, and interactions shape mechanical behavior and function. Systems of interest include structural proteins, fibrous networks, and flexible materials.

Physics-informed AI and multiscale simulation

This research combines physics-informed machine learning with multiscale simulation, experimental validation, and design optimization to establish structure–property relationships and support the rational design of material systems and structures.

01 Manufacturable design

AI-guided design of manufacturable fibrous networks

This work addresses continuous fiber paths and topological constraints in the AI-guided design of manufacturable fibrous networks. It connects topology generation, finite element analysis, graph neural networks, and reinforcement learning, with validation using 3D-printed specimens to link structural designs to physical materials.

REFINe · Nature Communications · 2026

Read the paper ↗
Research question, methods, and results+
Research question
How can mechanical performance and continuous manufacturing requirements be considered together during design, so that digital topologies can become weavable and printable structures?
Methods
The REFINe framework uses the TOPNet algorithm to maintain continuous fiber paths. Automated finite element analysis, physics-inspired graph neural networks, and reinforcement learning support mechanical response prediction and topology optimization, followed by mapping planar networks onto curved surfaces.
Results and validation
The optimized structures reported in the paper achieve approximately 50% greater strength and 20% lower mass than the initial designs. Experiments use specimens fabricated by stereolithography (SLA) and fused deposition modeling (FDM).

02 Physics-informed learning

Predicting deformation and mechanics in disordered fibrous networks

He contributed to research on physics-informed graph neural networks that incorporate network connectivity and physical information to predict deformation and stress–strain responses in disordered fibrous materials, supporting rapid mechanical assessment and structural design.

GNMP · Nano Letters · 2026

Read the paper ↗
Research question, methods, and results+
Research question
Sparse connectivity and nonuniform deformation make the mechanics of disordered fibrous networks difficult to predict. How can network structure be used to rapidly predict nodal deformation and the overall stress–strain response?
Methods
The GNMP approach combines graph attention message passing, multiscale physical embeddings, and a bond-length-guided scheduling strategy to describe nonaffine rearrangements in two-dimensional semiflexible networks.
Results and validation
Predictions are compared with molecular dynamics simulations, while experiments on 3D-printed networks examine deformation patterns and structural descriptors, supporting rapid mechanical assessment of fibrous networks.

03 Open-source tools

FiberNet / FiberScope

Open-source projects for fibrous network design, simulation, and intelligent optimization. FiberNet provides Python computational tools, while FiberScope offers an interactive research interface, making structure generation, mechanical analysis, and inverse design easier to use and reproduce.

Source on GitHub ↗ Try FiberScope ↗ Project overview →
Workflow and tools+
Research workflow
Bringing structure generation, simulation, analysis, and design into one workflow supports both batch studies in Python and interactive exploration through a graphical interface.
Tools and methods
FiberNet provides interfaces for 2D and 3D network generation, beam-frame finite element analysis, structural descriptor analysis, and searches against target curves. FiberScope provides structure editing, trajectory viewing, and geometry export.
Examples and outputs
Public examples cover network generation, saving and reloading simulation results, extracting structural features, and constructing continuous paths on planes and curved surfaces. Source code and documentation record the parameters and outputs for each step.
